## Global locks
- Same room, wardrobe, Milo identity and spatial logic across narrative opening/payoff.
- I00–I08 camera locked frontal. No orbit, perspective flip, cutaway or invented insert.
- Wide starts screen-left at S0; narrow starts screen-right. Strand identity never changes.

- Every shot has a defined first frame, last frame, reference set and QA gate.
- Any topology error fails regardless of visual quality.
- Do not spend video-generation credits until reference assets and generation packets are complete.
