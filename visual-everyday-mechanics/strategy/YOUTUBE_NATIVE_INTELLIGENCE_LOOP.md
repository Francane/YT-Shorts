# YouTube Native Intelligence Loop

Purpose: complement external Opportunity Mining with YouTube Studio's own search/audience signals after launch.

## Rules
- Trends/Research and content-gap signals are discovery evidence, not proof of zero competition.
- Initial Video Ideas are hypotheses, not a fixed 60-video calendar.
- Search terms, Suggested Videos, audience-watched content and replay/retention are inputs to the next Opportunity decision.
- Do not automatically create a video for every mismatched search phrase or replay peak.
- Use native Test & Compare when available; do not reduce evaluation to CTR alone.
- Timing data becomes useful only when the channel has enough audience data.

## Loop
External research → YouTube native signals → Opportunity → VEM filters → production → publish → Search/Suggested/Audience/Retention signals → next Opportunity.

## Launch rule
Publish a small initial sample first. Reassess family/topic priority after approximately 5–10 videos using whatever channel-native evidence is actually available.

## Immediate priority
This does not block Pilot 001. The current production gate remains the renderer bake-off and completion of the Four-in-Hand pilot.
